1. What We Collect

When you interact with Cordia AI via SMS, we collect your mobile phone number, the content of messages you send, and the date and time of each message. We also record your consent to receive SMS messages when you first contact the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Message content is processed by an AI language model (Anthropic Claude) to generate responses. We retain conversation history to provide continuity across sessions. Your phone number is used solely to route SMS replies back to you. We do not use your information for marketing or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. SMS Communications

Cordia AI sends SMS messages only in response to messages you send first. This is a conversational service — no unsolicited outbound messages are sent. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance.

4. Data Retention

Conversation data and consent records are retained for a minimum of 4 years for compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below.

5. Security

Data is stored in encrypted databases hosted on U.S. cloud infrastructure. Access is limited to authorized personnel only. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

SMS delivery is handled by Twilio, Inc. AI processing is handled by Anthropic, PBC. Both services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not share personally identifiable information with these providers beyond what is required to operate the service.
